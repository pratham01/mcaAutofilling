# MCA Form AOC-4 Direct Browser Auto-Filler Extension

This Chrome Extension runs **directly inside your standard Chrome browser session**, providing a 100% human-authenticated, zero-bot-detection alternative to automate Form AOC-4 filling.

---

## 🚀 How to Install in 30 Seconds

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. In the URL bar, go to:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions/
   ```
3. Toggle on **Developer mode** (top-right switch).
4. Click the **Load unpacked** button (top-left).
5. Select the `extension` folder located in:
   ```text
   e:\AutomateFormFIll\extension
   ```
6. The extension **"MCA AOC-4 Form Auto-Filler"** is now installed!
   *(If you previously loaded it, just click the **Reload (🔄)** circular icon on its card in `chrome://extensions/`).*

---

## ⚡ How to Use

1. Log into the MCA portal as normal in your Chrome browser:
   `https://www.mca.gov.in/content/mca/global/en/foportal/fologin.html`
2. Open Form AOC-4:
   `https://www.mca.gov.in/content/mca/global/en/mca/e-filing/annual-filings/form-aoc4.html`
   *(If the tab was already open before loading/reloading the extension, refresh it with **F5**).*
3. Notice the sleek floating **⚡ AOC-4 Direct Auto-Filler** widget in the bottom-right corner of the page:
   - Click **"1. Fill Section 1 (Part A)"** to populate Company Information, CIN, Statutory Dates, AGM Details, and Auditor details.
   - Click **"2. Fill Section 2 (Balance Sheet)"** to populate Share Capital, Reserves, Borrowings, Payables, Assets, and Provisions.
   - Click **"3. Fill Section 3 (Profit & Loss)"** to populate Revenue, Expenses, Depreciation, and Profit After Tax.
   - Or click **"🚀 One-Click Fill All Sections"** to populate all 3 sections in one shot!
   - Click **"💾 Save Draft (Global Toolbar)"** to persist the draft directly to your MCA account!
